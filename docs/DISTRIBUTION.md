# Releasing your tracks on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music & more

MusicAI exports **distribution-ready masters**: 44.1 kHz / 16-bit stereo WAV —
exactly what every distributor asks for. MP3 (320 kbps) and FLAC downloads get
your title, artist name, album, genre and cover art embedded automatically.

## 1. Make sure you own what you release

- **ACE-Step engine** — code *and* weights are **Apache-2.0**. Outputs have
  **no watermark** and no platform claiming rights over your track. You can
  release commercially. ✅
- **Demo synth engine** — placeholder music for testing the app; nothing
  stops you from releasing it, but it's not real AI music. Always generate
  release tracks with ACE-Step (locally or via the Replicate engine).
- Your **lyrics are yours** — write original lyrics (or use lyrics you have
  rights to). Never generate imitations of a real artist's voice.

> Note: distributors and stores have AI-content policies that change often.
> Most (e.g. Spotify via distributors) accept AI-assisted music but require
> you to hold the rights and to not impersonate real artists. Check the
> current policy of your distributor before uploading.

## 2. Prepare the release in MusicAI

1. Generate the track with the **ACE-Step** engine.
2. Set **title** and **artist name** before downloading.
3. Upload **cover art** (stores require 3000×3000 px JPG/PNG, RGB, no URLs,
   no social handles, no misleading brand names on the artwork).
4. Download the **WAV** master — upload this file to your distributor.

## 3. Pick a distributor

You can't upload directly to Spotify/Apple — you go through a distributor:

| Distributor | Cost model | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **DistroKid** | ~$23/year, unlimited uploads | High volume, fast releases |
| **TuneCore** | Per-release or yearly plans | Established artists |
| **Amuse** | Free tier available | Getting started at zero cost |
| **RouteNote** | Free (85% royalties) or premium | Zero-budget releases |
| **LANDR** | Yearly plans + mastering tools | All-in-one polish + release |
| **CD Baby** | One-time fee per release | Set-and-forget releases |

All of the above deliver to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Amazon
Music, Instagram/Facebook, TikTok, JioSaavn, Gaana, Wynk and more.

## 4. Upload checklist

- [ ] WAV master (44.1 kHz / 16-bit) — direct from MusicAI ✅
- [ ] 3000×3000 cover art
- [ ] Final title, artist name, genre, release date
- [ ] Lyrics (many stores show synced/static lyrics)
- [ ] Declare AI usage if the distributor asks (be honest — it's allowed)

## 5. After release

- Claim your **Spotify for Artists** and **Apple Music for Artists** profiles.
- Register with a royalty collection society if you monetize seriously.
- Keep your generation prompts/seeds — they're proof of authorship.
